The why behind the ♻

Waste less.
Make more possible.

Recycling isn’t the whole answer—but when we reduce and reuse first, it’s a practical way to conserve resources and give materials another useful life.

🌳 17

mature trees can be saved for every ton of paper recycled.

⚡ 95%

less energy is used to make aluminum from recycled material than from raw bauxite.

🧡 1 small

sorting habit helps keep valuable materials in use and can inspire a whole household.

Sources: U.S. EPA, “Paper Recycling” and “Aluminum: Material-Specific Data”; International Aluminum Institute, “Aluminium Recycling.” Figures are approximate and can vary by process.

A little behind-the-scenes magic

From “done”
to “what’s next?”

Recycling is a team effort. Your part is simple: sort it right. Then local collectors and processors do the rest.

01 / AT HOME

Empty & sort

Give containers a quick empty. Separate the materials your local program accepts.

02 / COLLECTION

Pick-up day

Recyclables are collected and taken to a sorting facility—not straight to a new product.

03 / SORTING

Find their match

Equipment and people separate materials, remove contaminants and prepare them for processing.

04 / NEW LIFE

Back in the loop

Processed material can become feedstock for new products. The loop starts again.
